Your Privacy Matters

At Savvy, protecting your personal information is one of our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide our savings platform and improve your experience.

  • Name and contact information.
  • Email address and phone number.
  • Account credentials.
  • Payment and transaction records.
  • Device, browser, and log information.
  • Communications you send to our support team.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Process contributions and payouts.
  • Verify your identity.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve platform security.
  • Send important service notifications.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers, payment processors, identity verification partners, regulators when legally required, or during a business merger or acquisition.

4. Data Security

Savvy uses industry-standard technical and organizational safeguards including encryption, secure authentication, monitoring, and access controls to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

5. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, improve performance, and understand how our services are used.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your account where permitted by law.
  • Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Object to certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.

8. Children's Privacy

Savvy is not intended for individuals under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will revise the "Last Updated" date and notify users where required.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the Savvy support team through the contact information provided within the application.
